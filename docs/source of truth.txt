Yes, this is very doable, I've built similar extraction and diffing pipelines before. Here's how I'd approach the full scope:

	•	Crawler: Playwright for JS-rendered sites, BeautifulSoup for static ones. Each of the 50 sites will likely need its own selector config, since gov sites rarely share a template.
